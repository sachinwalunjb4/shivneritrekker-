Our story

From weekend fort hikes to Himalayan summits

{{ site.name }} started in 2014 as a group of friends from Pune who spent every monsoon weekend exploring the forts of the Sahyadris. Friends of friends kept asking to join, and soon we were running a weekend batch every Saturday.

Today we run more than 300 departures a year, from one-day waterfall treks near Mumbai to Everest Base Camp in Nepal. We still lead every trek ourselves, keep groups small and treat the mountains and their villages with respect.
